# How to Use You Inc. in Exam Week (Classmate Survival Guide)

## The Core Idea
You don't have time to re-read 400 slides per course before tomorrow morning's exam or case discussion.
**You Inc.** treats your MBA degree like an operating company:
- Your concepts are **capital assets**.
- Forgetting is **depreciation** (modeled by the mathematical FSRS algorithm).
- Revision sessions are **reinvestments** that protect your department share price.

---

## 3 Steps to Walk In Prepared

### 1. Upload Any Class Deck
- Go to `/decks` and drag in your professor's raw slide deck (`.pptx` or `.pdf`).
- In ~45 seconds, the system parses the core economic & strategic frameworks, creates flashcards linked to exact slides, and indexes concepts into your Bahi-Khata ledger.

### 2. Run a 10-Minute Morning Reinvestment Sprints
- Open `/review`. The queue lists only cards at risk of depreciating today based on your memory half-life.
- Hit **Space** to flip. If you're stuck, tap **"Slide Context"** to jump directly to the professor's source diagram without leafing through downloads.
- When an answer is graded, your concept asset valuation updates immediately.

### 3. Drill a 5-Minute Cold Call in the Boardroom
- Navigate to `/boardroom`.
- Click **"Cold Call Drill"** or select **"PM / Consulting Mock"**.
- The AI interviewer poses a Socratic prompt directly from your lecture concepts and pushes back with realistic follow-ups. You get structured feedback on **MECE framing, unit economics, and executive presence**.

---

## Exam Night Checklist
- [ ] Upload this week's slides to your Department library.
- [ ] Complete all cards in the `/review` queue until your ledger says **"Reinvestment Complete"**.
- [ ] Inspect `/boardroom` &rarr; **"Exam Plan"** for high-risk formulas (e.g., WACC, ROIC, TAM).
- [ ] Walk into the exam room with verified recall accuracy, not caffeine-fueled cramming.
